NEBULA QUEST

Survive the nebula and defeat the Alien Queen.

BLUE SHIP: Fast and evasive. Your only ship at launch.

PURPLE SHIP: Auto-fire weapons. Unlock it by surviving the first hunt.

RED SHIP: Kinetic shield. Unlock it by defeating the second miniboss.

SPYDA: Destroy nest pods with the purple weapon before they hatch random enemies. Dodge marked spores, escape spreading webs, and survive her laser-to-lane combo to expose her. Later phases add more pods and hazards.

QUEEN: Dodge her continuous weaving blood-bullet patterns, marked eruptions, sweeping blade, and the clone's aimed burst. The arena closes in each phase, and her core is exposed after each combo.

Survive to earn score until the Warden. After its trial, earn points only from enemies and energy. Switch unlocked ships with SPACE, a mouse click, or the touch button.

Controls: Choose your setup then play.
Mouse only (smooth follow) or Keyboard only (direct WASD/Arrow movement). Press ESC to pause; use Settings at the top right to adjust the game.
